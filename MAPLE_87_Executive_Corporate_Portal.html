Corporate Profile

Dual-Entity Synergy & Regional Delivery

MAPLE 87 is the shared brand presenting the combined construction capabilities, civil engineering expertise, and project execution track record of two incorporated entities operating seamlessly across Singapore and Malaysia.

Established in Singapore in 2010 with Maple87 Pte. Ltd. and expanded into Malaysia in 2016 through Maple87 Builders Sdn. Bhd., the group handles projects ranging from major public housing precast installations to high-specification institutional healthcare facilities, data centers, and multi-unit landed housing developments.
